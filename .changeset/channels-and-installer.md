Install with one line on macOS and Linux: `curl -fsSL https://polyphemus.ai/install.sh | sh` installs Node too if yours is missing or too old, needs no sudo, and `poly update` keeps it current. Follow beta releases with `poly update --channel beta`, and go back with `--channel stable`. About shows which you follow, and links to polyphemus.ai.
