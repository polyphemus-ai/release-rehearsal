A project's Work tab answers "where is this" on a phone, not only on a wide screen. It opens with where the project stands — the handoff its agents rewrite at the end of every turn, which every session starts from and which no person could see until now — then what's waiting on you, what's working, what's stopped, what's proposed and what runs on a schedule, and then its threads. On a wide screen the list stays in the sidebar where it already was.
