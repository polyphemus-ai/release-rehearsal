What agents made for a project is kept together. A page, a table, a document or a chart used to exist only inside the thread that produced it, so finding one meant remembering where you'd asked for it. A project's Work tab now lists what's been made, newest first, each saying which thread it came out of and opening the way it always did — sandboxed, for an HTML page. Pictures a workflow took of its own pages stay where they belong, as evidence on their step.
