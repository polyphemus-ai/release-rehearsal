An agent at work wears one comet going round it like a ring round a planet, and nothing else. The orbit sits just outside the mark and crosses its face, so the comet passes over the front on the near half and disappears behind on the far half, swapping at each edge. It's a real traveller — a bright head with a short tail behind it, dimmer as it goes round the back. The mark itself keeps still now and blinks now and then: it used to yaw, bob and spin a full turn as well, which on a 24px badge was three things competing at once.
