Sign-ins agents' browsers keep: on the Browser connection, sign in to a site by hand in a live view of a browser on Polyphemus's computer, from your phone or desktop, and Polyphemus keeps the cookies the site sets in the vault. Agents' browsers in the projects you pick start out signed in; no model sees your password or the cookies. A sign-in is yours alone to place, and it's held back in any project where someone besides you could see what agents do there.
