Grok Build's plan usage is shown: Polyphemus asks xAI's billing endpoint every 5 minutes with the Grok CLI's own sign-in (read-only, never stored or refreshed) and shows the week's percent used and when it resets on its card. When xAI says there's room, an earlier quota error ends straight away. It's not a documented xAI API, so the card says where the figure comes from, and why it's unknown when a lookup fails.
