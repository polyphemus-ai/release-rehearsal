Finance is in Connect a service: link your banks through Plaid and let an agent read balances, transactions, investments and bills in a project you grant it to. Read-only — nothing there can move money. It runs inside Polyphemus rather than as a server, so your Plaid keys and each bank's token stay in your vault and are never handed to a model, an agent or a subprocess. Set up a Plaid app of your own once; Sandbox works immediately, real banks need Plaid to approve your account.
