Agents can propose a routine: scheduled work Polyphemus runs in the project, which shows under Waiting on you with its schedule and what it does each time, and exists only once you accept it. A proposed routine asks first or only reads — never one that runs without asking. Claude Code's own session schedulers are turned off in threads Polyphemus runs, since they end with the turn and never fire.
