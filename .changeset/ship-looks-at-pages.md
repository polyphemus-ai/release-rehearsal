Shipping a website looks at what it built: the plan names the pages a change affects, and every round poly serves the site from its worktree and opens each page at phone and desktop widths in headless Chrome. A page that doesn't load, answers an error or throws sends the round back; the pictures show on the step, go to the reviewer, and are pointed to when you're asked to merge. Needs Chrome or Chromium (`POLYPHEMUS_CHROME` if it's somewhere unusual).
