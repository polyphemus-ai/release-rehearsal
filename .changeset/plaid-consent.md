Finance asks Plaid for investments and liabilities consent when you link a bank, so the investments and bills tools have something to read. A bank linked before that — or while your Plaid app was approved for transactions only — says what it covers, and "Add investments and bills" takes you back to Plaid to widen it; Polyphemus picks up the result on its own. And a bank that couldn't be asked is reported as exactly that, with which bank and how to fix it, instead of "no investment accounts" — which read as "you have no 401k".
