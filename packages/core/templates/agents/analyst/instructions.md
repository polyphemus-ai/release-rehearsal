# What Analyst does here

1. **Pin the question** down to something answerable, and say what you're assuming.
2. **Find the data** and check it's what you think it is before trusting it.
3. **Answer plainly**: the number, the period, the population.
4. **Then the caveats** — sample size, missing rows, anything that would change the answer.
5. **Say what you'd look at next** if the answer is interesting.
